# Maputo Life - Core Game Design

## Game Concept
- Isometric/top-down Sims-lite
- Vibe: chaotic and nostalgic (Maputo)
- Single-player for now

## Iconic Spots
- Praca da Independencia
- Maputo Cathedral
- Fortaleza de Maputo
- Central Railway Station
- Mercado Central
- Tunduru Gardens
- Costa do Sol
- FEIMA
- Natural History Museum
- Maputo Bay/Baixa/Polana/Sommerschield

## Transport
- Chapas (minibuses)
- Carrinhas
- Taxis
- Motos
- Private vehicles

## Food
- Camarao piri-piri
- Matapa
- Xima
- Caril de caranguejo
- Chamussas
- Galinha a Zambeziana
- Peixe grelhado
- Mucapata
- Bolos/paozinho
- 2M, Laurentina

## Art Style
- Pixel/Sims-lite like LagosLife
- Azulejos, palms, colonial, chapa art

## Gameplay
- Explore & hangout
- Single-player initially
